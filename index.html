about Mehyar Visuals

I’m Mehyar — a Melbourne-based photographer focused on capturing real, cinematic moments. I look for movement, light, and the small details most people miss.

I shoot locally and while travelling, creating images that feel natural, timeless, and honest — always with intention.

Whether you need LinkedIn/business portraits, event coverage, modelling/portfolio shots, or a standout Instagram photo — I’ll bring your vision to life with a refined cinematic style.
